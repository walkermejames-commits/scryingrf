# Connected devices

Scrying 0.3.1 prioritises the installed phone and explicit human consent. It profiles Android-exposed capabilities locally, keeps people records local, and exposes BLE/Wi-Fi observation only as explicit contributions. Each person can independently be permitted or denied device-health, BLE-observer, and Wi-Fi-observer sharing; this policy is stored locally and may be revoked at any time.

The current release does **not** claim multi-phone pairing, QR authentication, camera streaming, location sharing, or sensor streaming. Those require a reviewed secure LAN protocol and explicit per-person approvals. Nearby observations are secondary context and cannot enter a shared system without an ownership or permission confirmation.
